# Shelf photo counter: status update for Trade Marketing

Date: 27 October 2026. Work started 21 September (5 weeks so far).
DRAFT.

## The goal

Merchandisers already photograph shelves. Today someone then has to look at
each photo and count how many fronts are Kix-Max and TorshX and how many are
competitors. The app does that counting from the photo: the merchandiser
takes the picture and moves on, and the count goes straight into reports.

## Where we are, in plain terms

| What the app needs to do | Today |
| --- | --- |
| Find every product on the shelf | **Works.** Finds about 9 in 10 products. |
| Tell cans from glass bottles | **Works.** Right about 19 times in 20. |
| Tell whose product it is (ours vs each competitor) | **Not ready.** Right about 2 times in 3. Most mistakes are between similar-looking glass bottles. |
| Give a share-of-shelf figure you can trust | **Not ready**, because of the line above. |
| Accept photos and return counts | **Works** as a demo, about 5 seconds per photo. |

The hard part still ahead is telling the brands apart reliably. We know how
to improve it: teach the app with real shelf photos where people have marked
each brand. That is what the labeling team is doing now.

## The labeling work

- 3 people are marking products on 1,250 shelf photos. About a quarter
  (332 photos) is done after 4 working days.
- At the current pace, the rest takes about **2 more weeks**.
- Quality is mixed. Some photos were submitted without being checked; those
  are being sent back. We want to slip a few photos with known answers into
  each person's work, to see who is accurate.
- We do **not** need all 10,000 photos labeled. A few hundred good ones
  should be enough to start; the first training round will show how many.

## When merchandisers can use it

| Milestone | Expected |
| --- | --- |
| Labeling finished and checked | ~2 weeks |
| App trained on our own photos, brand accuracy re-measured | ~4 weeks |
| Demo with a real share-of-shelf number | ~4-5 weeks, if accuracy is good enough |
| Merchandisers using it day to day | After the demo; needs connection to the field app and IT sign-off |

These are estimates. Brand accuracy after training is the one thing we
cannot promise in advance.

## What it costs

- No purchases or licences so far; everything runs on company machines.
- The main cost is people's time: one engineer, plus the 3 labelers (about
  75 more hours of labeling for the current photos).
- No extra storage needed: the training computer has about 5 TB.

## Risks, in short

- **Brand accuracy may stay too low** for share of shelf. Then the first
  version reports product counts only, with brands added later.
- **Labeling quality.** Careless labeling teaches the app the wrong thing.
  Checks are being added.
- **One training computer.** It belonged to a colleague who is leaving; it
  must be handed over and backed up first.
- **Photo quality in the field.** Blurry or distant photos are counted less
  accurately. A short photo guide for merchandisers would help.
